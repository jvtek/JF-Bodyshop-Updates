JF BodyShop

Photo and job app for your panel beater. Install it on your Android phone in a minute.

Download for Android

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file (JFSoftware-Bodyshop-App.apk) from your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, tap Settings and allow installs from your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open JF BodyShop and sign in with the company code and login you were given.
Needs Android 10 or newer. If your phone shows a Play Protect warning, tap More details then Install anyway. Once installed, the app tells you when a new version is ready.
